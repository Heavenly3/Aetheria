## Description

**Aetheria** is an idle RPG you can leave running in a tab, or close and come back to later: your hero keeps working while you are away.

**Train 21 skills.** Mine ore, smelt bars, forge armour, cook, brew potions, craft runes, farm in real time and more. Every action has its own mastery, and maxed skills can be prestiged.

**Fight your way up.** Ten areas, seven bosses, multi-room dungeons, slayer tasks and an endless tower. Beyond the Abyss wait the Void Rift and the Celestial Spire, with aether gear and boss-only uniques.

**Run a tavern.** Hire staff with their own traits and levels who train skills for you in parallel, send adventurers on expeditions, deliver daily orders and try your luck at dice.

**Collect and grow.** 23 rare pets, gear enchanting from +1 to +10, daily and weekly tasks with a streak, achievements, and an Ascension tree that makes every new life faster.

**Plays anywhere.** Four save slots, offline progress, English and Spanish, and it can be installed as an app.
