Ultra rare finds, a tracking board that leads you where to go, and saves that are much harder to lose in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Tracking
- **One board for everything on the go:**
  - story quests
  - the slayer task
  - guild contracts
  - tavern orders
  - daily and weekly tasks
  - the next step of the story
- **Where it is:** at the top of Quests, and from the bar at the top of every screen, which shows your first objective and opens the whole board.
- **It takes you there.** Picking a line goes to the right screen and tab, scrolls to the exact action, creature, seed or shop shelf and makes it glow:
  - **an item to make or gather:** the action that makes it, or a creature that drops it if you cannot make it yet
  - **a creature to defeat:** the combat screen, in the right tab, at that creature
  - **a skill to raise:** the best action you can do right now
  - **something ready:** the quest, order or contract to hand in
- **Missing ingredients:** crafting goals list what you lack, each with its own link to go and get it.

### Miracles: ultra rare finds
Every skill action and every kill now rolls for 25 items far beyond anything that can be made.

| Tier | How rare | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Epic** | about 1 in 5,000 | Tools of tier 10 for mining, woodcutting, fishing and farming, and a charm for every other skill (+50% XP, +25% speed) that works just by being in your bag |
| **Legendary** | 1 in 5,000 to 1 in 50,000 | The Hourglass of Ages (+25% speed in everything) and gear from world bosses, dragons, the tower and the deepest areas |
| **Mythic** | **1 in 1,000,000** | Three weapons with twice the power of the best ever made, the Amulet of Endless Fortune and the Heart of Aetheria (double XP in everything) |

- **A moment of its own:** a find opens its own window with its tier, what it does and how rare it is.
- **Safe:** finds are locked so they are never sold by mistake, and they come with you when you are reborn.
- **The Miracles tab** in the Compendium shows all 25: what each one does once found, and until then where it drops and how rarely.
- **Four new achievements** go with them.

### Finding things
- **Where to get it.** Every item lists what gives it:
  - skill actions
  - creatures, bosses and dungeons
  - shops
  - chests
  - crops and more

  Each line shows the level or price it asks and the chance when it is not certain, and leads to its screen. Item tooltips name the first places too.
- **Search.** Find any screen, skill, item, creature or quest:
  - **How to open it:** the magnifier in the top bar, **Ctrl+K** or **/**.
  - **Accents and case:** they do not matter.
  - **Items you do not have:** they open in the inventory, so you can see where they come from.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:**

  | Key | Goes to |
  |---|---|
  | H | Hero |
  | I | Inventory |
  | C | Combat |
  | Q | Quests |
  | T | Tavern |
  | P | Pets |
  | , | Settings |
  | A | What you are doing |
  | ? | Every shortcut |

### Your saves
- **Automatic backups.** Every save keeps three copies in the browser:
  - a recent one
  - one from about an hour ago
  - one from about a day ago

  **Bringing one back:** from Settings → Save. A deleted game can be recovered from the title screen. Now and then the game reminds you to also keep a copy outside the browser.
- **Feedback.** A new Settings section to report a bug or share an idea.
  - **What it adds:** the version, your device and your hero.
  - **Where it goes:** you post it yourself on itch.io or GitHub; nothing is sent on its own.

## Changed

- **Settings** are organised in eight clear sections: game, display, sound, alerts, accessibility, save, feedback and about.
  - **New options:** text size, which pop-ups show, the next step bar and the welcome-back summary.
- **Loading:** a loading screen shows at once instead of a blank page.
- **The slowest skills are faster from level 60:**

  | Hours to 99 (no bonuses) | Before | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mining | 323 | 222 |
  | Runecrafting | 319 | 217 |
  | Agility | 315 | 223 |
  | Farming | 644 | 357 |

  Farming's late crops pay more XP per harvest, snapdragons grow faster and torstol can be planted from level 80.
- **One tab per game:** a game opened in a second tab runs there only, and the first tab steps aside after saving, so the two never overwrite each other.
- **New versions:** a game left open says when a new version is out.
- **Tutorial:** when what a step points at is off screen (often on phones), a “Show me where” button scrolls to it.

## Fixed

- **Regeneration:** creatures and bosses that regenerate healed 1.5% of their health every second, so the Aether Sovereign healed faster than any hero could hurt it. They now heal 0.6% a second of at most 400 health, and the time per kill shown in combat counts it. Trolls, hydras and astral golems fall at the pace of their neighbours.
- **A game that cannot start** shows a page to reload, download your saves and copy the error, instead of an empty screen. That file can be loaded back into the game.
- **Saving:** a save the browser refuses (storage full or blocked) is reported instead of being lost silently, and a broken save no longer blocks the title screen.
