The Aether Crown, the last three acts of the story, and a realm that reacts in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### The story comes to an end
- **Act IV** takes the journal to the frozen north and the dragon lands.
- **Act V** goes down into the Abyss.
- **Act VI** crosses the Void to the top of the sky.
- **New characters** for each act, plus new rumours at the tavern.
- **Three endings.** Each ending has its own achievement, and reading every act and ending earns more achievements and six new titles (48 in all).

### The Aether Crown
- **Seven shards** of the crown are hidden across the acts. Each one is found by beating the bosses that guard it.
- **Three sockets** take the shards you choose, each with its own power to use.
- **Choosing the ending.** Once the crown is whole, you choose how the story ends. Endings you have seen are remembered through ascension.

### Combat
- **Thirteen stances** across melee, ranged and magic, each trading accuracy, damage, speed, defence or XP in its own way. The setup screen compares them live before you pick one.
- **Tactics:**
  - Retreat before falling when the food runs out (no gold lost, hunting streak kept).
  - Stop when the slayer task is done.
  - Brace during a boss's big moves.
- **Five loadouts** that also keep the stance and its ability bar.
- **Boss moves.** Every boss charges big moves of its own, with a warning and a countdown in the arena: a crushing slam, fire breath, a frost nova, a venom spray or a mending that you can break by hitting hard. A guarding stance takes far less from them.

### A realm that reacts
- **Regional threat.** Every region grows restless when nobody hunts there, also while you are away, and calms down with every creature you hunt. It runs from calm through restless and dangerous to overrun.
- **Danger pays.** Troubled regions have stronger creatures, but they carry more gold and loot and bring more elites.
- **Bounties.** An overrun region is announced, and bringing it back to calm pays King Edmund's bounty.

### Routines
- **Rules for your hero.** Up to twelve rules of the form "when this happens, do that", tried from top to bottom while the game is open.
- **Conditions** cover being idle, item counts, the weather, day and night, omens, patrons, ready plots, slayer tasks and festivals.
- **Actions** train a skill, fight, serve, harvest, sell and more.
- **Respects your choices.** Rules wait a few minutes after you choose something by hand.
- **Eight ready-made rules** to start from.

### Techniques
Skills that used to be "pick the best action and wait" now each have a twist of their own:
- **Mining:** rich veins.
- **Woodcutting:** felled trees.
- **Smithing and Fletching:** momentum.
- **Cooking:** a steady hand.
- **Herblore:** focus.
- **Firemaking:** a hearth that keeps warming you after you leave.
- **Runecrafting:** runes that resonate with the weather and the night.

### The last levels
Every skill now has something new close to 99:
- **Woodcutting:** elder trees, with their bow, staff, rod and fires.
- **Fishing and Cooking:** the Starfall Shoals and the starfin.
- **Thieving:** the royal treasurer.
- **Herblore:** the aether overload.
- **Runecrafting:** the soul rune.
- **Prayer:** astral bones dropped beyond the Abyss.
- **Farming:** the aether lotus, a fifth hybrid and the finest food in the game.

### Sound
- **Four tabs.** Settings are split into general, sound, alerts and accessibility.
- **Thirty alerts.** Each can be turned off, given its own volume, previewed, heard with the tab hidden or sent as a browser notification.
- **Packs and profiles.** Three sound packs and six sound profiles, plus one of your own.
- **Music.** Thirteen music themes that follow the place, the weather and the fight.
- **Accessibility:** captions, visual alerts when muted, soft treble and mono sound.

### The purse
- **Every currency in one place.** The gold chip in the top bar opens a purse with all your currencies, where each one comes from and a link to where it is spent.

## Changed

- **Crafting:** robes and ranger hides give less XP, in line with the rest of the skill.
- **Combat setup:** the screen is rebuilt around stances, tactics and loadouts.
- **Phones:** the newer screens fit better, from the farm and the tavern to festivals and guilds.

## Fixed

- **Fishing:** the screen no longer picks a water you cannot fish yet.
- **Sound settings:** alert names show on phones.
