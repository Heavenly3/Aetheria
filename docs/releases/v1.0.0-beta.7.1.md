A small fix for the new combat screen in **Aetheria** 1.0.0-beta.7.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## Fixed

- **Ability bar:** the three slots now always fit in their panel. Each slot shows its priority number, the ability and its controls, on any screen size.
- **Weapon and style:** the style panel warns when the weapon in hand is made for another style (a staff with a melee style, for example) and offers to switch to the right one.

Everything new in beta 7 (abilities, the journal, music, weather effects and more) is in the [beta 7 notes](https://github.com/Heavenly3/Aetheria/releases/tag/v1.0.0-beta.7).

## Downloads

- `aetheria-web-v1.0.0-beta.7.1.zip`: the web build. Unzip it on any static host, or upload it to itch.io as an HTML game.
- `aetheria-v1.0.0-beta.7.1.html`: the whole game in one file. Download it and open it in a browser; no internet connection needed.

Saves from earlier betas keep working.
