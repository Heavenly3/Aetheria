Guilds, festivals all year round and a deeper version of almost every skill in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Guilds
- **Twelve guilds in every world**, each with its own name, crest, motto, guildmaster, members, tier (1–5) and focus: one role, a few roles, or open to all.
- **Joining:** meet a guild's requirements, pay its fee and pass the entry trial, a task plus a riddle, a rune sequence or a code lock.
- **Contracts** renew every four hours and pay guild marks, gold and reputation. Climb six ranks for a perk that grows with your rank and the guild's tier, and spend marks in the guild shop.
- **A ranking** orders the guilds by renown, which grows every day and with your own work.

### The story goes on
- **Act III** takes the journal to the fallen capital.

### Festivals and fairs
- **Four big festivals a year** (Spring Bloom, Midsummer Fires, Harvest Festival and Winter's Veil), each about three weeks long with its own bonus, shop and pet.
- **A festival track:** every token you earn is also a festival point that climbs a ten-step track, with the festive outfit at the end, and a ranking against the guilds.
- **An activity for each festival:** a flower contest grown on the farm in spring, a bonfire fed with logs and a festival fishing water in summer, a fair stand and giant vegetables in autumn, and the Frost Wraith and gifts for your friends from the journal in winter.
- **Weekend fairs:** on the second weekend of every other month, a fishing derby, a tower rush, a market day or a night of omens, each with its own rule and rewards.
- **Festivals reach the whole game:** three journal tales per festival, festival contracts in the guilds, seasonal guests at the tavern, a compendium page for festival keepsakes, and their own achievements and titles.

### Crafting and the compendium
- **Crafting quality.** Gear made at the anvil, the fletching bench or the crafting table can come out Fine (+5% stats), Superior (+10%) or Masterwork (+20%). The better you know the recipe, the better the odds.
- **New gear lines:** mage robes woven from flax in four cloths, and ranger hides stitched from wolf pelts, snake skins and wyvern scales, each with its own set bonus.
- **The compendium.** The bestiary becomes a book in three parts: creatures, every item you have held (twenty pages, each with a permanent bonus when full) and the masterworks you have crafted.

### Combat
- **Slayer, reworked.** Pick one of three hunts (easy, standard or hard). While a creature is your task, a tougher Superior version can appear and drop slayer sigils to imbue the slayer helm. Points buy permanent perks, blocked creatures and supplies.
- **Endless tower.** From floor 6, each block of five floors has a weekly twist that pays more tokens. Named guardians guard every tenth floor, and the guilds' best climbers race you to the top.
- **Every stat counts.** Dexterity speeds up attacks and adds crits, Vitality reduces damage, Luck adds crits, elites and finer fish, and new talents cover block, crits, attack speed, armour, elites, fishing luck and craft quality. The combat power panel shows where every number comes from.

### Church and agility
- **The Order of the Dawn:** a daily offering earns favour (longer blessings, an extra slot, cheaper blessings), holy water brewed with Herblore renews every active blessing, and an altar gives three times the Prayer XP for bones.
- **Agility:** courses also pay coin pouches and nests, and marks of grace buy the four-piece graceful outfit.

### The tavern bar
- **Patrons** drop in over time, even offline, and ask for food and drink from your stores. Serving them pays gold and tips and raises the tavern's reputation.
- **Special guests** ask for more and pay with rare goods.
- **Brews:** amber ale, herbal mead, strawberry wine and fire grog, made with Cooking.
- **Treasure maps** found while thieving open an expedition of their own.

### Gathering
- **Fishing waters:** five waters from the Dawn River to the Abyssal Trench, each with its own fish. Every cast lands a random catch, and bait, mastery of the water and luck make the finer fish likelier. A fishing scene shows the line, the bobber and your latest catches.
- **Thieving:** every theft raises the guards' heat, and getting caught on alert means jail. Valuables sell best at the fence, and four heists play out in stages: plan, slip in, crack and get away.
- **Farming:** compost and supercompost, bountiful crops and pests, a favourite season for every crop, hybrid crops recorded in an almanac, and a farmyard with a chicken coop, a beehive, a scarecrow and a well.
- **Rare finds:** geodes while mining, caskets while fishing, bird nests while woodcutting and seed pouches while harvesting. Plus a gem rock, tuna and anglerfish, thieving stalls, sickles and lockpicks.

### Progress
- **Achievements and titles** for guilds, the compendium, heists, the farm, the tavern, the tower, fishing and festivals: 42 titles in all.
- **Daily tasks and quests** for every new system.
- **Statistics** with records for every part of the game.
