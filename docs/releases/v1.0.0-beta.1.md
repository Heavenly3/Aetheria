The first public beta of **Aetheria**, a fantasy idle RPG for the browser.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)**: nothing to install. It can also be installed as an app and keeps working offline.

## What's in the game

- **21 skills** across gathering, artisan, support and combat, each with per-action mastery and prestige.
- **Hero level** with attributes and talents, six roles and four difficulties.
- **Combat**: 10 areas, 7 bosses, 6 multi-room dungeons, slayer tasks, an endless tower, prayers and loadouts.
- **Endgame**: the Void Rift and the Celestial Spire, aether gear and boss-only uniques.
- **Tavern**: staff who train skills for you in parallel, expeditions, daily orders, drinks and dice.
- **Collection**: 23 rare pets, gear enchanting up to +10, achievements, and daily and weekly tasks with a streak.
- **Ascension**: start over for Aether shards and spend them in a permanent upgrade tree.
- **Action queue and chained crafting**: missing materials are gathered and made automatically.
- **Offline progress**, four save slots and autosave.

## New in this beta

- Light theme next to the dark one, or follow the device setting.
- Phone layout with a bottom navigation bar and larger touch targets.
- Guided tutorial for new heroes.
- Save files and transfer links to move a hero to another device.

## Downloads

- `aetheria-web-v1.0.0-beta.1.zip`: the web build. Unzip it on any static host or upload it to itch.io as an HTML game.
- `aetheria-v1.0.0-beta.1.html`: the whole game in one file. Download it and open it in a browser, no internet needed.

This is a beta: balance may still change. Saves from this version will keep working in later ones.
