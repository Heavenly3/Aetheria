The biggest update yet for **Aetheria**: omens, seasonal festivals, a weekly boss, pets you raise yourself, and a new shop and inventory.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Weekly boss
A new challenge for heroes of combat level 30 and above. Every week, Monday to Sunday, a different boss arrives. Every player meets the same one.

- **Six bosses.** Each one shrugs off one combat style, is weak to another and has a mechanic of its own:
  - **The Stone Colossus** hardens its skin.
  - **The Mire Hydra** regrows its wounds.
  - **Morwen, the Lich Queen** raises thralls to shield her.
  - **The Ashen Wyrm** breathes fire.
  - **The Leviathan** throws damage back at you.
  - **Rimeheart** freezes you and slows your attacks.
- **Its wounds stay all week.** Every attempt adds to the damage dealt before.
- **Attempts last up to five minutes.** The boss's fury grows the longer you fight, and falling to it costs no gold.
- **Damage milestones** (10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and the kill) pay gold, gem chests, stardust, starlight shards and relics.
- **Exclusive trophies.** The first kill of each boss drops its own piece of gear, from the Colossus Bulwark to the Shroud of the Lich Queen.

### Raising pets
Pets are no longer just a lucky drop.

- **Eggs.** A lucky find now arrives as a mysterious egg that hatches in your incubator after half an hour.
- **Companion.** Choose one pet as your companion and give it a name.
  - **Feeding.** Each pet has a favourite food: ores for the golem, fish for the heron, bones for the raven… Any cooked food will also do.
  - **Hunger.** Only your companion gets hungry, and only slowly.
  - **Petting.** Pet it once an hour to grow your bond.
- **Levels 1 to 20.** A fed companion earns a share of the skill XP you earn. Every pet's bonus grows with its level.
- **Bond, from Wary to Soulbound.** A closer bond brings:
  - a gift basket filled with things your pet likes;
  - bigger gifts;
  - a stronger companion bonus.
- **Companion bonus.** A fed companion doubles its bonus.
- **Top bar.** A companion chip shows its hunger and tells you when gifts or eggs are waiting.

Pets you already own keep working and can be raised from level 1.

### Omens
Random events have been replaced by twelve rare world phenomena that arrive without warning.

- **Signs.** A cryptic sign comes first. Then, depending on the omen:
  - a Starfall or a Blood Moon;
  - an Arcane Eclipse or a Comet of Wishes;
  - a Gilded Goblin to chase, or a Wandering Rift with a horror that scales with you;
  - a Veiled Caravan.
  - The rarest is the Eye that Opens.
- **Rewards found nowhere else:**
  - seven omen pets;
  - relics of random quality;
  - permanent wishes;
  - a boon rolled when an omen ends.
- **Offering.** Offer stardust to call the next omen sooner.
- **Chronicle.** The Omens screen records the omens you have witnessed. The rest stay a mystery.

### Seasonal festivals
Four festivals run on their real dates:

| Festival | Dates |
|---|---|
| Harvest | October |
| Winter's Veil | 15 December to 15 January |
| Spring Bloom | 20 March to 20 April |
| Midsummer Fires | 21 June to 21 July |

Each one has a passive bonus and its own tokens. Its shop sells an exclusive pet, a title, a portrait and a colour.

### Titles and appearance
- Pick a title to show next to your hero's name. There are 29, earned through achievements, omens, festivals and now pets and weekly bosses.
- Change your portrait and colour at any time.

### Shop and inventory
- **Two tabs in the shop.** The shop has a Buy tab and a Sell tab. Selling now happens only at the shop.
- **Sale warnings.** The shop warns you before you sell:
  - your equipped ammunition;
  - your combat food or potion;
  - rare items;
  - very large sales.
- **More categories.** The inventory has 21 categories in four groups, plus a search box.
- **Compare before you equip.** Selecting gear or a tool compares it with what you wear, two-handed weapons included. Materials show what they are used for.
- **Confirm before you take off gear.** Taking off equipment or a tool asks first and tells you what you will lose: set bonuses, combat bonuses or a tool in use.

### Side menu
The side menu is now split into collapsible sections, and Settings stays pinned at the bottom.

### Achievements
New achievements for raising pets, reaching the deepest bond and slaying weekly bosses, with new titles to match.
