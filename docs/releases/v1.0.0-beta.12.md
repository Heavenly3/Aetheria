Hero cards to share, a record of every life you have lived, and miracles that are truly rare in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Hero cards
- **A picture of your hero.** The Hero screen makes a card to share or keep:
  - name, title, class and difficulty
  - hero, total and combat level
  - play time and rebirths
  - the four best skills
  - every miracle found, the rarest first
- **A card for every miracle.** A find gets its own card with its tier, its odds and who found it, from the find's window or from the Compendium.
- **Share, download or copy.** The card is a 1200×675 image drawn on your device; nothing is uploaded until you share it yourself.

### Past lives
- **Every life written down.** Each rebirth records the life that ended:
  - how long it was played
  - total and hero level
  - bosses beaten and creatures slain
  - the best skill
  - the shards it gave
  - a trial lived through
  - the miracles found in it
- **On the Rebirth screen:** the life you are living sits on top with its numbers so far, and the lives before it follow, newest first. A life that ended quicker than the one before says by how much.
- **Older games:** lives lived before this update are counted but cannot be listed, since nothing about them was kept.

### Luck streak
- **Long dry spells soften.** Each epic and legendary find keeps its own count: the longer it is overdue, the likelier it gets, up to three times its chance. Finding it starts the count again.
- **It lasts:** the streak survives rebirth, and the Compendium tells you when your luck with a find is growing.
- **Mythic finds** stay pure luck.

## Changed

- **Miracles are far rarer.** Finding one should feel like an event:

  | Find | Before | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | Epic tools and charms | about 1 in 5,000 | about 1 in 50,000 |
  | Hourglass of Ages | 1 in 50,000 | 1 in 250,000 |
  | Mythic | 1 in 1,000,000 | 1 in 1,000,000 |

  With the streak, half of all players find a given epic within about 23 hours of training its skill, and nine in ten within about 57.
- **Magic costs about what arrows do.** A new mage used to run out of gold within hours.
  - **Spells:** every spell takes one rune of its element and one catalyst.
  - **Staffs:** a staff makes air runes free while you hold it.
  - **The shop:** basic runes cost 2 gold and chaos runes 12.
  - **New mages** start with mind and water runes.
- **The tracker suggests what your hero can actually do.**
  - **An item you cannot make yet:** it trains the skill up to the level the item needs, instead of sending you to a creature far too strong for you.
  - **A crafting skill to train:** it points at the cheapest recipe and lists the missing ingredients with where to get them.
- **Lighter on slow phones.** Busy screens refresh what only needs to look current every second or two, tooltips are built only when you reach them, and numbers are formatted once per language:

  | Phone-speed CPU | Before | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | Combat | 24% busy, 42 fps | 3% busy, 56 fps |
  | Hero | 21% busy, 41 fps | 5% busy, 57 fps |

- **Easier to read and to use:**
  - **Contrast:** faint text, inactive tabs and toggles reach a 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.
  - **Locked cards** turn grey with a dashed edge instead of fading their text away.
  - **Screen readers:** quantity fields, view toggles, sliders and the weather chip have names, and action cards no longer nest one button inside another.
  - **Keyboard:** a “Skip to content” link comes first, and changing screens moves focus to the new screen.
- **Keeping your saves:** the game asks the browser to keep its storage when it clears space. Firefox is left alone, since there that shows a prompt.
- **Spanish** names things the same way everywhere (sin conexión, eterio, renacimiento, conjunto, pedidos for tavern orders) and writes percentages and quotes one way.

## Fixed

- **A Third Crop:** the farming quest about hybrid crops had the same name inside the game as Green Thumb, so it shared that quest's progress and could never be finished. It stands on its own now, and the achievement for every quest can be earned.
- **Quest rewards** that open an area or a boss showed a raw text instead of its name.
- **Broken numbers in saves:** a value that went wrong in a calculation was written as an empty one and broke the next load and every backup after it. Saving now puts it back to its default, saves that already had one are mended when loaded, and either case is noted for bug reports.
- **Spanish wording:** the tutorial asked for a bronze item by the wrong name, the welcome tips asked to press a button that does not exist, and kill counts read as deaths.
