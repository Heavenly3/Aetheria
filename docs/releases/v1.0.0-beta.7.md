A new way to fight, the start of the story, music and weather that matters in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Combat
Fights are still automatic, but your gear, levels and choices now show in every blow.

- **Weapons with their own pace.** Bows are quick, hammers slow but heavy, staves cast steadily and air spells cast faster. Every weapon has a crit chance, and some leave a status on the target.
- **Statuses** for both sides: bleeding, poison (stacks up to three times), burns, stuns, slows and weakness, each shown with its timer.
- **Defence that you can feel.** Agility lets you dodge blows, a shield can block half of one, and armour now soaks part of every hit, up to 40%.
- **Creature traits.** Every creature has one to three: venomous serpents, armoured golems, regenerating trolls, life-drinking vampires, frenzied wolves, elusive goblins… They show in the arena and the bestiary.
- **Elites.** Now and then a Fierce, Armoured, Swift or Ancient version of a creature appears in the open field, much tougher but worth 4× gold, 2.5× drop chances and stardust.
- **Boss phases.** Bosses turn enraged at half health and desperate at a quarter, hitting harder and faster.
- **Hunting streaks.** Every 25 kills in a row without dying add +2.5% combat XP and loot, up to +25%. Dying breaks the streak.

### Abilities
- **Six abilities per combat style**, unlocked by Attack, Ranged or Magic level: strong strikes, multi-hits, statuses, a heal and timed boons like Berserk, Evasion or Mana Shield.
- **Energy** builds with every attack, critical hit and blow taken.
- **A bar of three.** Pick and order your abilities in the Combat screen; they fire on their own, and the first slot has priority. Healing waits until you are hurt.

### Combat power
- A **combat power** panel on the Hero screen: damage per second, max and average hit, attack speed, hit and crit chance, defence, chance to be hit, dodge, block and damage reduction.
- **Comparing gear** shows how power, damage per second, accuracy and damage taken would change, and every equipment tooltip sums it up.
- **Monster cards** show the time per kill and how risky the fight is.
- **The arena** shows live damage, kills, XP and gold per hour, and a short log of the fight.

### The journal
- **The story of Aetheria**, told in short chapters that unlock as you explore, defeat bosses and witness omens: the prologue and the first two acts.
- **Characters** you meet along the way, with a different cast of names in every new game.
- **Memories** of past lives, one with each ascension.
- The tavern keeper talks about your story, the astrologer leaves notes on the omens you witness, and studied creatures reveal their lore in the bestiary.

### Music and ambient sound
- **Music** made by the game itself, with no audio files: calm while you train, adventurous, dark, cold, ominous or celestial depending on where you fight, slower and darker at night.
- **Ambient sound** that follows the weather: rain, wind, thunder, birds by day and crickets by night.
- **Sound settings** with volumes for music, ambience and effects, silence while the tab is in the background, and a mute button in the top bar.

### Weather that matters
Every weather, season and the night now change the game. Rain helps fishing and farming, fog helps thieving, wind helps woodcutting, a heatwave speeds up smithing, and storms boost magic but make monsters 20% stronger. The weather icon in the top bar lists every effect, and each change is announced.

### Update notes
A **What's new** window after each update, and the full list of updates in Settings.

## Changed

- **Combat screen:** the fight comes first, with tabs below for style and abilities, supplies and loadouts. The style panel shows your weapon's pace, crit chance and effect.
- **Food:** new heroes start with their food set, and when one food runs out you move on to the best one left.
- **Settings:** new options for fewer pop-ups, full numbers instead of short ones and reduced animations.
- **Tutorial:** it now explains energy and abilities and ends in the journal.

## Fixed

- **Herblore** can be trained from level 1, and the shop sells guam next to the vials of water.
- **Notifications** no longer show up more than once.
