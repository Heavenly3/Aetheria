A fix for playing **Aetheria** on itch.io, together with everything new since beta 2.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## Fixed

- The game can now be scrolled when it is embedded in another page, such as on itch.io. Before, anything that did not fit in the game frame could not be reached. The main panel, the side menu and the title screen now scroll on their own.

## New since beta 2

### Equipment sets
Wearing several pieces of the same set grants extra bonuses. There are 13 sets:

- **Metal armour** for every metal, from bronze to aether: defence with 2 pieces, melee accuracy and damage with 4, and extra max HP with the full set of 5.
- **Leather hunter** and **Dragonhide** for ranged fighters, and **Apprentice's regalia** for mages.
- **Slayer's garb**, **Dragonbane** and the endgame **Regalia of the Ages**.

Active set bonuses are listed on the Hero screen, and every piece shows its set in the item details.

### Bestiary
A new screen that records every creature you defeat: 43 entries across the ten areas, the dungeon lords and the world bosses.

- **Seen** (first kill): stats and weakness.
- **Studied** (25 kills, 10 for bosses): every drop and its chance.
- **Mastered** (250 kills, 50 for bosses): +5% accuracy and damage against that creature.
- Study every creature in a group to claim gold and a small permanent bonus.
- The bestiary is kept when you ascend, and kills from before this update already count.

### Achievements
Six new achievements: Naturalist, Living Encyclopedia, Master Hunter and Dressed for the Part.

## Downloads

- `aetheria-web-v1.0.0-beta.4.zip`: the web build. Unzip it on any static host or upload it to itch.io as an HTML game.
- `aetheria-v1.0.0-beta.4.html`: the whole game in one file. Download it and open it in a browser, no internet needed.

Saves from earlier betas keep working.
