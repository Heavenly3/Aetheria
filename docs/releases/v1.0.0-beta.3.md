New content for the **Aetheria** beta: equipment sets and a bestiary.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Equipment sets
Wearing several pieces of the same set now grants extra bonuses. There are 13 sets:

- **Metal armour** for every metal, from bronze to aether: defence with 2 pieces, melee accuracy and damage with 4, and extra max HP with the full set of 5. Stronger metals give bigger bonuses.
- **Leather hunter** and **Dragonhide** for ranged fighters.
- **Apprentice's regalia** for mages.
- **Slayer's garb**: the slayer helm and cape together.
- **Dragonbane**: the dragon blade and shield.
- **Regalia of the Ages**: the four endgame uniques.

Active set bonuses are listed on the Hero screen, and every piece shows its set, the pieces you wear and the next bonus in the item details.

### Bestiary
A new screen that records every creature you defeat: 43 entries across the ten areas, the dungeon lords and the world bosses.

- **Seen** (first kill): stats and weakness.
- **Studied** (25 kills, 10 for bosses): every drop and its chance.
- **Mastered** (250 kills, 50 for bosses): +5% accuracy and damage against that creature.
- Drops are discovered as they fall.
- Study every creature in a group to claim its reward: gold and a small permanent bonus, different for each area.
- The bestiary is kept when you ascend, and kills from before this update already count.

### Achievements
Six new achievements: Naturalist, Living Encyclopedia, Master Hunter and Dressed for the Part.
