A map of the realm, a company of companions at your side, voices for every character and a tavern full of life in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### A map of the realm
- **Combat is a map.** Every region sits on one board that can be dragged, zoomed with the wheel, a pinch, buttons or keys, opened full screen and centred on the hero. It opens close in on where you are fighting.
- **A page for every region:** its places, its dungeon and its bosses, with fights shown right there instead of on a separate screen.
- **Everything has a place.** The map marks where the hero is fighting and where the story, quests, the slayer task and guild work are waiting. The town, the Endless Tower and the weekly boss arena are on it too, and the tracker, journal, search and item sources lead to the right region.
- **Preparation window.** Style, abilities, supplies, tactics, loadouts and the company in one window, opened from every place where you fight.

### The company
- **Companions hired for good,** one of each trade. Up to two come along to every boss fight and dungeon for a wage:

  | Trade | In a fight |
  |---|---|
  | Squire | Draws blows and takes part of charged strikes |
  | Archer | Often cuts off a creature's move |
  | Mage | Counts double towards breaking a healing |
  | Cleric | Mends the hero and lifts bleeding, poison and burns |

- **They grow with you.** They level up with every kill, earn trust, fight better once loyal and stay through rebirth. If the hero falls they need ten minutes to recover.
- **Five companions met along the way.** Four join through the story and one through the guild's trust. Each is better at their trade and brings a small bonus outside the fights. At full trust they ask for help with a quest of their own, and finishing it makes their trade stronger. They have a voice in the journal, with words that change as trust grows.
- **The War Banner,** a new rebirth upgrade, lets a third companion come along.

### Creature moves and tactics rules
- **Creatures fight back.** Every creature past the meadow's chickens and cows charges a move of its own:
  - strikes that bleed, poison, burn or stun;
  - a frenzy or hardened armour;
  - fading out of reach or regrowing;
  - a thief that pilfers gold and runs off unless it falls in time.

  Elites bring one more. Stunning or slowing a creature while it charges cuts the move off. Moves show as unknown until you have seen them.
- **Tactics rules for abilities,** all on by default:
  - answer a creature's move at once with an ability that stuns or slows;
  - defend with Second wind, Mana shield or Evasion below a chosen health;
  - break an enemy's healing with the hardest-hitting ability.

### Voices
- **Every character speaks** in short sung syllables made with WebAudio. There are no audio files and no real words, so it sounds the same in both languages. Each person has a timbre of their own, questions rise and statements fall, and the words appear in step with the voice.
- **Who speaks:**
  - a narrator reads new journal pages, and any page can be heard again;
  - bosses speak when the fight begins, at each phase and when they fall;
  - quest givers tell their quests and thank you when they are done;
  - the tavern keeper greets you back;
  - every person in the journal can be talked to, and what they say grows with the story.
- **Settings:** voices have their own channel, volume and reading speed, and the music steps back while someone speaks.

### Guild affairs
- **Affairs instead of contracts,** now and then. They are followed up across the realm through their leads: creatures, skills and a word with someone from the journal. Each one ends with an enigma:
  - deduction;
  - a cipher;
  - mirrors and light beams;
  - an astrolabe;
  - a false coin on the scales;
  - an echo of runes.
- **Fair puzzles.** Every enigma is made for your save and always has an answer. A guild mate can help for marks, and wrong answers cost time.
- **Entry trials:** each guild now tests those who would join it with the enigma it favours.

### Character creation and origins
- **Creation step by step:** class, appearance, difficulty, origin and begin.
  - Classes show how they fight and their full starting kit.
  - Difficulty is told in plain words from its real numbers.
  - The last step sums up the hero.
- **Origins:** where the hero comes from, the one thing they remember when they wake.
  - Six origins bring a small head start, and the wanderer brings only the mystery.
  - Each adds a line of its own to the journal's first page.
  - Older games are wanderers.

### Expedition crews
- **Crews, not lone adventurers.** Adventurers and companions set out together: two at first, three with a third-level tavern and four with a fifth-level one. Every extra hand adds to the crew's level and haul.
- **Every trade helps on the road:**

  | Trade | On the road | Turns away |
  |---|---|---|
  | Adventurer | +5% success | Wolves |
  | Squire | +4% success, nobody comes back hurt | Ambushes |
  | Archer | +25% loot | Wolves |
  | Mage | Rare finds more often | Roadside spirits |
  | Cleric | +10% success | Fevers |

- **Things happen along the way:** one event for every four hours out. It might be a hidden cache, a pedlar, some ruins, a shrine or a good camp, or a mishap the right trade can turn away.
- **Home ground:** each companion met along the way knows one destination and does better there.
- **Trips that keep going:** a trip can set out again on its own when it returns, or be called back early.
- **Companions on the road** earn trust and experience, and sit out fights until they are back.
- **A 12-hour trip** joins the 1, 4 and 8-hour ones.
- **The tab** shows the destinations beside a crew planner, the trips under way above and a chronicle below of who went, what happened and what they brought home.

### Staff that grows
- **Seasoned workers:** at levels 25, 50 and 75 a worker sheds a bad trait, or learns a good one if they have none.
- **Promotions:** a worker can be raised to the next rarity for gold and tavern tokens once they reach the level for it.
- **At a glance:** the staff tab shows how many are working, idle, stalled, away or hurt, and the wages per hour.

### Tavern games
- **Eight tables**, one at a time from a row that remembers the last one played:
  - **The King's Twenty-One** against the keeper, with a deck of coins, cups, swords and staves;
  - **dice**;
  - **darts** on a real board;
  - **knucklebone**;
  - **arm wrestling** up a ladder of seven rivals, from the farmhand to the dwarf king, where the top rungs need real Strength;
  - **a drinking contest**, where Hitpoints help;
  - **three men's morris** against the keeper;
  - **the rune shell game**, where Runecrafting helps.
- **One layout for every table:** a wide felt where the game is played, and a side panel with the bet and quick buttons, the actions, the table's record and the last results. Every game is animated, and reduced motion turns the effects off.
- **Bets** go up to the tavern's limit, and the keeper is called by the name your world gave them.

## Changed

- **Mercenaries are gone;** the company takes their place.
- **The top bar** stays on two rows below 1280px wide, with health and gold first, and skill filters scroll with the page instead of covering the actions.
- **Enigmas** no longer name how hard they are, and the achievement for settling affairs alone no longer asks for the hardest ones.

## Fixed

- **Pet bonuses** spilled out of their cards. Each bonus is now its own tag.
- **Inventory marks:** the protected, in-use and market marks sat behind item icons and were barely visible. They are now small badges on the tile's corners.
