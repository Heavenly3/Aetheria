Echoes of the bosses, trials for your next life, weekly records and a gentler start in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### After the story
- **Echoes.** Once you have chosen an ending in the Crown, the seven world bosses come back as echoes: stronger, quicker with their big moves and richer.
  - **A lasting reward:** the first time you beat each echo, it stays beaten in every life and gives **+1% XP and +1% gold for good**, up to +7%.
  - **A title:** beating all seven earns *the Echo Hunter*.
- **Trials.** When you ascend, you can choose a rule for the next life:

  | Trial | Rule | Lasting reward |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Fasting** | You cannot eat | +15% healing, +3 max HP |
  | **Arcane** | Magic only | +5% magic damage and accuracy |
  | **Self-made** | The shop sells you nothing | +6% gold |
  | **Frail** | Half your usual health | +5% defence |

  - **Completing a trial:** live the life through until you can ascend again. That ascension pays ×1.5 shards and the trial's reward is yours for good.
  - **A title:** completing all four earns *the Unbroken*.
  - **Changing your mind:** you can give up a trial at any time.
- **Weekly records.** Statistics shows this week next to last week and your best week ever:
  - creatures defeated
  - gold earned
  - actions
  - slayer tasks
  - highest tower floor
  - damage to the weekly boss

  Beating your best week is announced once a week.

### A gentler start
- **A menu that grows with you.** A new hero starts with the basic screens. The rest show up as they become useful:
  - **With the tutorial:** the tavern, guilds and compendium.
  - **With combat level:** slayer and the tower.
  - **With total level:** the home, church and routines.
  - **Omens:** after the first one you witness.

  Each new screen is announced with a line on what it is for and marked as new until you open it. Each menu section says how many screens are left to discover and what opens them.
- **Next step.** Once the tutorial is over, a line at the top of every screen shows the one next thing worth doing (a quest ready to hand in, the ending to choose, or the next page of the story) and takes you where it is done. It can be hidden until the goal changes.
- **Your choice.** When creating a hero you can switch off the guided tutorial and the growing menu. Settings can skip the tutorial or show every screen at any time.
- **Existing saves:** they keep every screen open.

## Changed

- **Offline progress** is worked out up to three times faster. Long absences show a progress bar instead of a frozen screen.
- **Phones:**
  - The top bar fits on one sliding row, with health and gold first.
  - The tabs at the top of a screen stay on one row.
