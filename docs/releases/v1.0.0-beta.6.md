A relic forge, weather, in-game help everywhere and a friendlier interface for **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://heavenly3.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Relic forge
The Forge has a new **Relics** tab for working the relics you get from omens and the weekly boss.

- **Reforge** a relic to roll its quality again. The exact chance of every quality is shown before you pay.
  - Seal it with a starlight shard and its quality cannot drop.
  - Every reforge that does not improve the relic heats the forge, which makes better qualities more likely on the next roll. Improving a relic cools the forge again.
  - A relic you are wearing is reforged without taking it off.
- **Fuse** three relics of the same quality into one of the next quality. You choose its kind: amulet, cape or shield.
- **Reshape** a relic into another kind and keep its quality.
- The hammer falls, then the result is revealed.
- New achievements and the Starsmith title.

### Weather
The weather changes every three hours, and every player sees the same weather at the same time. It follows the real seasons.

- **Everyday weather:** clear skies, starry nights, overcast skies, rain, fog, wind and snow.
- **Climate events:** rarer thunderstorms, heatwaves and blizzards. A message announces each one when it begins.
- **In the top bar:** an icon shows the current weather. Hover it to see the season, when the weather changes next and whether a climate event is on its way.
- Weather does not affect the game yet.

### Omen signs
Each omen now announces itself with its own cryptic sign. The same sign stays in the chronicle, next to the omen's rarity, until you witness that omen, so you can try to guess what each sign means.

### In-game help
- **Screen guides:** a "?" next to every screen title explains what the screen is for and how to use it.
- **Section hints:** the main sections of every screen have their own "?", and so do combat settings, gear stats and the numbers shown during a fight.
- **Item cards:** hover any item to see its card with kind, quality, stats, healing, effects, requirements, set, sell price and how many you have. Drops and chests also show their chance.
- **Touch screens:** tap a "?" to open it, tap anywhere else to close it.

### Shop
- **Buy tab:** now has filters like the Sell tab.
  - Sections, a search box and "I can afford it" and "I can use it" toggles.
  - Sorting by section, price or name.
  - Each item shows any level you still need, and a Max button fills in as many as your gold buys.
- **Sell tab:** the day's market is now the first panel.

### Interface
- **Activity bar:** you can fold it into a small bubble in the corner. The bubble shows the current action, its progress and the queue size. Tap it to open the bar again; the game remembers your choice.
- **Settings:** moved into the hero menu, at the top right.

## Changed

- **Festivals are turned off for now.** Festival bonuses, tokens and the festival shop no longer appear. Festival pets, titles and cosmetics you already own are kept.

## Fixed

- **Tooltips** no longer close one frame after they open.
